When needed, evidence-based surgical interventions offer a path to long-term health and renewed vitality.

Who is a Candidate for Surgery?

Surgery is a major medical decision. It is recommended when other methods haven't provided the necessary results or when specific medical conditions require intervention.

Weight Related

  • • BMI Criteria met
  • • Obesity-related health issues
  • • Previous unsuccessful treatments

General Surgery

  • • Symptomatic Gallstones
  • • Hernias (Inguinal/Ventral)
  • • Appendicitis
  • • Severe Acid Reflux

The SCOD Safety Standard

Your safety is our absolute priority. We combine surgical expertise with world-class infrastructure to ensure the highest standards of care.

Expert Multidisciplinary Team

Your care is managed not just by a surgeon, but by a team including anesthesiologists, cardiologists, and specialists.

Advanced Technology

We utilize 4K/3D laparoscopic systems and the latest energy devices to ensure precision, minimal blood loss, and faster healing.

Strict Safety Protocols

Adherence to international ERAS (Enhanced Recovery After Surgery) protocols to minimize pain and hospital stay.

Hospital Infrastructure

Operated in high-volume, accredited centers with dedicated surgical setups and ICU backup for absolute safety.
